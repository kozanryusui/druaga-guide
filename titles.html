Player titles

The seven sections follow the seven tabs in the Tower. Each section keeps the game’s order of 24 titles. A title can have a different name for each character.

Insert your character card in the Tower after you meet a requirement. Once the Tower awards a title, you keep it. Item and Gold requirements check what you have when the Tower reads your card.
